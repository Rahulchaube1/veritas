\section*{Follow-up experiment with software v0.2.0}
\label{sec:followup}
\noindent The original study found two failures upstream of the decision rule. A tight verification budget could admit only copies, and a verifier could predict the right scientific verdict without citing a matching rationale. We revisited both with the same document collections.

\paragraph{Recovering distinct and linked observations.}
We prioritized distinct document texts and temporal states within a candidate prefix of at least 32. The conflict became visible in all 15 copy-count and budget settings, up from seven. Both systems inspected two documents in the one-step, sixteen-copy setting. A primary source beyond the candidate prefix can still be missed. A separate typed rule for English and Spanish counts raised the evidence-verdict score from 300 to 330 on the same 360 synthetic cases. All 30 remaining misses ask about a depot while the evidence establishes a station. Changing that single type in a separate diagnostic recovered 30 of 30 verdicts with both premises cited. Of 180 decisive answers on the original cases, 150 agreed with the simulator's latent truth.

\paragraph{Scientific verification under a fixed retrieval budget.}
We evaluated all 172 public SciFact development claims outside the earlier 48- and 80-claim neural audits. Each profile received the same document IDs per claim, with a budget of three. The revised profile reranks sentences and applies a larger, FEVER-trained entailment model to at most two eligible sentences per document. Table~\ref{tab:followup} shows the counts. A citation match requires the right document, stance and a cited span contained in an annotated rationale sentence.

\noindent Twenty-three verdicts improved and fourteen regressed (two-sided exact paired test, $p=.188$). Accuracy among decisive answers fell from 66.7\% to 64.2\% as coverage rose from 19.2\% to 30.8\%. Both models and the public development split limit generalization. The linked study supplies per-claim decisions, character offsets, matching gold sentence indices and commands to reproduce the comparison.\par
