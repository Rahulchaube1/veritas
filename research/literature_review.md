# What the literature establishes

This review addresses three design decisions for the release. Should a retrieved page vote on a claim? Which pages could represent the same observation? When is an answer worth giving? The source index and BibTeX file record the primary publications consulted. The search was targeted. It cannot establish priority or freedom to operate.

## Finding candidate passages

PageRank exploits the structure of hyperlinks. BM25 weighs lexical matches using term frequency, collection frequency and document length. DPR learns separate question and passage encoders. ColBERT uses contextual token representations and late interaction, and ColBERTv2 compresses those representations. Each offers a way to find candidate evidence. VERITAS evaluates the candidate list against independently supplied relevant document IDs. [Brin and Page](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0169-7552(98)00110-X) · [BM25](https://doi.org/10.1561/1500000019) · [DPR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.04906) · [ColBERT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12832) · [ColBERTv2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.01488)

Reciprocal rank fusion combines ordinal rankings without requiring their scores to have common units. RankNet learns an ordering from preference data. Our trained relevance component uses pointwise binary logistic loss over annotated and sampled document pairs. Approximate nearest-neighbour indexing uses HNSW in the neural configuration. BGE-M3 is an alternative multilingual retriever from the literature. [RRF](https://plg.uwaterloo.ca/~gvcormac/cormacksigir09-rrf.pdf) · [RankNet](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/learning-to-rank-using-gradient-descent/) · [HNSW](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.09320) · [BGE-M3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03216)

## Deciding what constitutes corroboration

Dong and colleagues used Bayesian source-dependence analysis in truth discovery. Their example of a false value spreading through copying directly anticipates the motivation in the project brief. Knowledge-Based Trust models a source’s trustworthiness using extracted factual triples and distinguishes extraction errors from source errors. Broder’s document-resemblance work supplies a foundation for shingle comparisons. In VERITAS a shared sentence or publisher can have several explanations. Graph edges carry their basis and a flag indicating whether a vote cap was applied. [Source dependence](https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol2/vldb09-pvldb47.pdf) · [Knowledge-Based Trust](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03519) · [Document resemblance](https://doi.org/10.1109/SEQUEN.1997.666900)

Temporal information retrieval includes document time, query time and temporally relevant results. HyTE conditions knowledge-graph embeddings on time. FreshLLMs introduces FreshQA and FreshPrompt for assessing and answering questions about changing facts. LiveFact evaluates evolving, incomplete evidence. We require explicit fact-validity intervals for time-scoped answers because an archive publication date alone does not establish when the assertion held. [Temporal IR survey](https://doi.org/10.1145/2619088) · [HyTE](https://aclanthology.org/D18-1225/) · [FreshLLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.03214) · [LiveFact](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.546/)

SNLI frames natural language inference as entailment, contradiction and neutrality. SciFact supplies scientific claims with abstract-level verdicts and sentence-level rationales. AVeriTeC includes real-world claim verification with web evidence. CONFACT explicitly evaluates conflicts from sources with different credibility. These tasks motivate measuring exact citation grounding alongside a claim verdict. [SNLI](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.05326) · [SciFact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.14974) · [AVeriTeC](https://doi.org/10.52202/075280-2842) · [CONFACT](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2025/1073)

## When to retrieve and when to stop

Self-RAG retrieves and critiques through self-reflection. Corrective RAG evaluates retrieval quality and takes corrective steps. FLARE retrieves during generation, and GraphRAG uses graph-based summaries for global questions. Search-R1 trains search-using models with reinforcement learning. Contextual-bandit news recommendation and information value theory motivate weighing expected gains against observation costs. Our stopping policy has a finite action budget and hand-set thresholds. We have no learned policy or identified counterfactual reward for a search not taken. [Self-RAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11511) · [CRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15884) · [FLARE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06983) · [GraphRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16130) · [Search-R1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.09516) · [Bandits](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.0146) · [Value of information](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSSC.1966.300074)

FActScore and SAFE evaluate long-form answers through atomic factual claims. SAFE introduces a search-assisted evaluator and LongFact prompts. ALCE evaluates citation quality. WebGPT studies browser-assisted answers with references and human feedback. Constitutional AI studies feedback guided by principles, a different supervision target from gold passage relevance. The FACTS Grounding task checks grounding against supplied context. The subsequent FACTS suite also evaluates search, parametric knowledge and multimodal reasoning. Our local evidence verdict and citation-offset checks cover a narrower task. [FActScore](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14251) · [SAFE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18802) · [ALCE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14627) · [WebGPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.09332) · [Constitutional AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073) · [FACTS Grounding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03200) · [FACTS suite](https://deepmind.google/blog/facts-benchmark-suite-systematically-evaluating-the-factuality-of-large-language-models/)

Guo and colleagues studied confidence calibration, including temperature scaling. Conformal prediction gives coverage under defined assumptions including exchangeability for standard split conformal prediction. SelectiveNet jointly optimizes prediction and rejection. VERITAS evaluates risk at different levels of coverage. Its exported isotonic model estimates correctness conditional on a decisive answer in the synthetic population only. [Calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.04599) · [Conformal prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07511) · [SelectiveNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.09192)

PoisonedRAG studies knowledge corruption in retrieval-augmented systems. Data Voids documents how sparse search results can be manipulated. A missing contradiction can mean that a corpus lacks adequate coverage. VERITAS exposes how many documents it examined and keeps source reliability and retrieval completeness unestimated. [PoisonedRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07867) · [Data Voids](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/data-voids-where-missing-data-can-easily-be-exploited/)

## Benchmark selection and name collision

BEIR compares diverse retrieval tasks. FEVER supports evidence-backed claim verification on Wikipedia, and FEVEROUS includes structured evidence such as tables. Rothermel and colleagues have already introduced **VeriTaS**, a dynamic multimodal benchmark for automated fact-checking. Their name expands to *Verified Theses and Statements*. This repository implements Rahul Chaube’s independent text search project and labels its own generated cases as synthetic. [BEIR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.08663) · [FEVER](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05355) · [FEVEROUS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.05707) · [ACL VeriTaS](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1948/)
